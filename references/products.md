# Products

What a project sells besides its tiers: one-time add-ons and DLC, consumables (gem packs, credits), add-on subscriptions. Anyone signed in can buy one unless it requires tiers. Docs: https://docs.yard.sh/v1/platform/projects/products.md (types, requirements, settings, selling, holdings, fulfillment, giving, limits). A product never grants access to the project or changes the buyer's tier (the `X-Yard-*` headers and `"access": "users"` look at tiers only), and buyers need a Yard account. Plan gate: `.team_permissions.max_products` (a plain `400` names the limit).

## Types, keys, requirements

| `type` | Pays | Gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `one_time` | once | the product while they meet its requirement; bought once |
| `consumable` | per purchase, 1-99 at a time | whatever your app delivers; bought again and again, each purchase waits to be fulfilled |
| `subscription` | `price_cents` monthly, or yearly with `yearly_discount_percent` | the product while it runs, on its own schedule |

- `key` (same grammar as tier keys) is permanent: checkout, holdings and webhooks name it. A published key cannot be renamed and keeps its type for good.
- `requires`: tier keys, any one is enough; empty means anyone signed in, tier or not, so a release can sell products with no tiers. Every required key must be a tier of that release, and a required tier cannot be removed. Holding a tier means bought, claimed free, subscribed or granted; a trial or a past-due subscription does not count. Holdings follow the product's requirement as the scope's release sells it now, not as it was at purchase: adding one applies to past buyers, dropping one restores them. Not meeting it leaves a `one_time` product `active: false`, sets a `subscription` product to end at period end (restored if met again first), and changes nothing for consumables already bought.
- Files: a `one_time` or `subscription` product can unlock release files when the release's `downloads.access` rules name its key (releases.md, https://docs.yard.sh/v1/platform/library/download-access.md). Its buyers then download those files from the Library and through license-key updates while the product is active and its requirement is met (a subscription product stops unlocking as soon as its required tier is gone), with or without a tier. A consumable cannot unlock files. A product a rule names must be in the release, and it cannot be removed or made consumable until no rule names it.
- Price 99 to 1,000,000 cents, always full price (no coupons, gifts, trials or launch discounts). A `one_time` or `consumable` product may be free (`price_cents: 0`): checkout takes no card and completes at once, and a free consumable left unfulfilled for 3 days is cancelled like a paid one is refunded. A `subscription` product can't be free. Icons are optional: exactly 256x256 PNG, JPEG or WebP, at most 1 MiB (only the dashboard resizes).

## Setting up

Products belong to a release, like pricing. The settings.json `products` block (project-files.md) is applied by `yard push` and GitHub sync: present, the release's products match it exactly (`[]` removes all), each `icon` path (relative to the directory holding `.yard`) uploads when its bytes change, a product without `icon` loses its icon, and a declared icon missing locally fails the push. Absent, products stay dashboard-managed.

`yard projects products <slug> [--release <tag|id>] --json` lists them; `add <slug> --spec <file|-> [--icon <path>]`, `edit <slug> <key> [--spec <file|->] [--icon <path> | --remove-icon]` (partial) and `rm <slug> <key> [--yes]` (buyers keep it) follow the same draft and `--release` rule as tiers (pricing.md).

## Selling and holdings

Signed-in visitors who meet the requirement buy from a landing page with `data-action="checkout" data-product="<key>"` (landing-pages.md), or from a checkout link (checkout.md: `product`, `quantity`, `interval`, `return_to`, `for`, `sandbox`). The return parameters are a hint to refresh, never proof of payment: read the holdings.

Holdings have one shape everywhere: `{ tier_keys: ["pro"], products: [{ key, name, type, requires, transaction_id | subscription_id, active, status, quantity?, acquired_at, requirement_met }] }`. Products the team gave are in it, indistinguishable from bought ones; only the API key reads add whether each was given and by whom. Use a product while `active`, and read `requirement_met` rather than comparing `requires` with `tier_keys`. For a product not held yet, offer it when its `requires` is empty or `tier_keys` holds one of its keys. Each unfulfilled consumable purchase is its own entry until it is fulfilled.

Read them from a project page or the landing page with `GET __yard/products` (relative URL; `{ authenticated, tier_keys, products }`; `503 products_unavailable` means retry, not "holds nothing"), from a page script with `window.yard.ownership()` (`.tier_keys`, `.products`), from an app outside the project with a Yard Auth token (https://docs.yard.sh/v1/api/yard-auth/get-user-products.md), or from a server, a service's server code included (the edge strips the browser session), with https://docs.yard.sh/v1/api/users/project-user-products.md and `products:read` (`?sandbox=` for a sandbox; name the user by their id or email; `404` is no such user, and one holding nothing gets empty lists), or with https://docs.yard.sh/v1/api/users/user-products.md when only the user's id is known (the id names its project; sandboxes left out). API keys are Pro-only, so on Basic use the page or token routes.

## Delivering consumables

Each consumable purchase waits unfulfilled until your app delivers it. Find it (holdings with `status: "unfulfilled"`, the `product.purchased` webhook, or https://docs.yard.sh/v1/api/fulfillments/list-pending-fulfillments.md), grant `quantity` times the product keyed on `transaction_id` so a retry never grants twice, then fulfill by `transaction_id`: `POST __yard/fulfillments/{transaction_id}` as the signed-in visitor, https://docs.yard.sh/v1/api/yard-auth/fulfill-user-purchase.md with a token, or https://docs.yard.sh/v1/api/fulfillments/fulfill-purchase.md with `products:fulfill`. Fulfilling twice returns the purchase unchanged; `409 purchase_refunded` means take the grant back; `409 purchase_not_paid` means try later. Fulfill only after a grant your own trusted code made. A purchase still unfulfilled 3 days after payment is refunded automatically, sandboxes included, and `product.refunded` fires with `refund_reason: "unfulfilled"`. A consumable the team gave waits and is fulfilled the same way but is never refunded: it waits until fulfilled or removed, a removed one answers `404` when fulfilled, and a fulfilled one can no longer be removed.

- Sandbox: product checkout is simulated. `__yard/products` and `__yard/fulfillments` on a sandbox's pages act on that sandbox, the API routes take `?sandbox=` (a service reads the name from `X-Yard-Sandbox`), and webhooks carry `sandbox`.
- `yard dev`: one `buyer:<product key>` persona per product holds it, plus the first tier it requires if it requires any (none qualifying means no buyer); a consumable's buyer has one unfulfilled purchase that stays fulfilled until restart. No webhooks fire, nothing is refunded after 3 days, and the team API still reaches Yard, where personas do not exist (local-dev.md).

## Giving products

A team can give one of the project's products to a user at no charge, from the dashboard only: the user's sheet on the project's Users page (or a sandbox's), Products tab, Manage Products. No CLI command, MCP tool or API key can give or remove one, so tell the user where it is. Giving needs a team role that can write and a plan that can sell projects; removing needs only the role. Docs: https://docs.yard.sh/v1/platform/projects/products.md, section Giving products.

- What it becomes: a `one_time` product held as if bought; 1-99 of a `consumable`, waiting to be fulfilled; a `subscription` product that is never billed and runs until the team removes it or until an optional end date (the end of that day, UTC). With an end date its holding reads `cancel_at_period_end: true` and `current_period_end` is that date; without one it has neither. The team can change or clear the date later, which sends no webhook or email, so read the holdings for the current one.
- Refused when the release the project or sandbox serves doesn't sell the product, the user already holds the one-time or subscription product (bought or given), doesn't meet its requirement (checkout's rule), or is a deleted project account. Allowed in pre-order, though nobody can buy until launch. A consumable can be given again and again.
- Held like a bought one: every holdings read lists it, it unlocks files, and the user can't buy a one-time or subscription product they were given. It follows its requirement: a given one-time product or subscription is `active: false` while the requirement is unmet, and a given subscription is never set to end for it. The holder's Library lists the project while they hold it (never in purchase history or receipts), and the holder can't cancel or resume a given subscription.
- Never a sale: no transaction, receipt, earnings, payout, subscriber count or product sales and holders; a given consumable does count as a pending delivery.
- Only what the team gave can be removed: a one-time product or subscription at once, a consumable only until it is fulfilled. Nothing is refunded.
- The user is emailed when a product is given or removed, and reminded 3 days before a given subscription's end date; never from a sandbox. `product.granted` and `product.grant_ended` fire, sandboxes included with `sandbox` set (webhooks.md).
